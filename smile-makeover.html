Smile Makeover

A personalised combination of cosmetic treatments designed to transform your smile — and give you the confidence to share it with the world.

What Is a Smile Makeover?

A smile makeover is a tailored treatment plan that combines two or more cosmetic dental procedures to improve the overall look of your smile — addressing colour, shape, alignment, and more in one coordinated plan.

Rather than a single fixed procedure, a smile makeover is designed around you. Depending on your goals, it may include teeth whitening, veneers, crowns, tooth-coloured fillings, gum reshaping, or replacing missing teeth.

Dr Deepesh Agrawal assesses your teeth, gums, and facial features to create a natural-looking result that suits your face and personality — giving you a smile that feels authentically yours.

What a Smile Makeover Can Fix

A smile makeover can address several concerns at once, creating a balanced, harmonious result:

  • Stained or discoloured teeth Brighten dull or yellowed teeth for a fresh, healthy look.
  • Chipped or worn teeth Restore teeth that are cracked, chipped, or worn down over time.
  • Gaps and spacing Close unwanted gaps for a more even, complete smile.
  • Uneven or misshapen teeth Reshape teeth that are too small, pointed, or irregular.
  • A "gummy" or uneven gum line Reshape the gums to create a more balanced smile.
  • Missing teeth Fill gaps with natural-looking replacements for a complete smile.
100% Every smile makeover is fully personalised — designed around your face, goals, and budget so the final result looks completely natural and uniquely yours.

Caring for Your New Smile

Protecting your investment keeps your makeover looking its best for years. A few simple habits go a long way:

  • Brush twice a day and floss daily to keep veneers, crowns, and natural teeth healthy.
  • Avoid staining foods and tobacco to keep whitened teeth and restorations bright.
  • Don't use your teeth as tools or bite very hard objects that could chip restorations.
  • Wear a night guard if advised to protect your smile if you grind your teeth.
  • Keep up regular checkups and cleanings so we can maintain your results over time.

Smile Makeover FAQs

What exactly does a smile makeover include?

It's a personalised combination of cosmetic treatments — which may include whitening, veneers, crowns, tooth-coloured fillings, gum reshaping, or replacing missing teeth. Your plan uses only the treatments that suit your goals.

How long does a smile makeover take?

It depends on the treatments involved. Some makeovers are completed in a couple of visits, while more comprehensive plans may be staged over several weeks. Dr Agrawal will outline a timeline for you.

Will my new smile look natural?

Yes. Every makeover is designed to complement your face, so the result looks beautiful yet believable — never fake or overdone.

Is a smile makeover painful?

Most cosmetic procedures are comfortable and carried out with local anaesthesia where needed. Any mild soreness afterwards is temporary and easily managed.

How long do the results last?

With good care and regular checkups, results can last many years. Veneers and crowns are durable, and whitening can be maintained with occasional touch-ups.

Can the treatment be spread out over time?

Absolutely. Many patients stage their makeover to suit their schedule and budget. We'll help you prioritise the treatments that matter most to you.
